# Portfolio Research Summary

> Compiled: 2026-09-17 | Sources: Gemini Deep Research, DeepSeek, Google Search

## Market Context — Gothenburg 2026

- 18,000 tech professionals needed annually in Sweden through 2028

- Average time-to-hire: 41-45 days

- Recruiter spends 7.4 seconds on initial screen

- Gothenburg = automotive, industrial, enterprise SaaS, healthtech

## Copy Strategy

- Lead with OUTCOME not process

- Swedish culture: lagom (balance) + prestigelös (no ego)

- Never: "move fast", "lone wolf", "vibe coder", "10x developer"

- Always: team fit, humility, learning mindset, deliverables

## AI Tooling — Conflicting Advice

- DeepSeek: keep AI tooling row, Lovable is ok in Sweden

- Gemini: remove AI tooling, Lovable = red flag

- Decision: keep but add qualifier explaining human oversight

## Title — "Product Engineer"

- DeepSeek: correct title, used in Swedish job posts

- Gemini: risky for junior/self-taught, use "Fullstack Developer" instead

- Decision: keep for now, monitor interview feedback

## What Swedish Employers Trust (ranked)

1. Live apps with real users

2. GitHub with real commit history

3. Architectural decisions explained

4. Lighthouse scores

5. Screenshots (lowest trust)

## What to Never Say

- "Built solo in record time"

- "I take full ownership" (sounds boastful)

- "AI-native" (signals dependency)

- "Vibe coding"

- Skill percentage bars

## Strongest Portfolio Elements (confirmed)

- Echo2000 production crisis story (24h rebuild, 129 users)

- Klar 100/100 Lighthouse Accessibility

- DJ Lobo — paid client, live, customer happy

- Methodology section (4-step AI workflow)
